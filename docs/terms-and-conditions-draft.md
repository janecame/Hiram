## 1. Introduction

These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern access to and use of Hiram (the "Platform," "we," "us"), a peer-to-peer item rental marketplace operating in the Philippines. Hiram connects two types of users:

- **Borrowers** — users who request to rent an item from another user.
- **Listers** — users who list their own items for other users to rent.

A single account may act as both a Borrower and a Lister. By creating an account or using the Platform, you agree to these Terms.

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Borrower** | A user who submits a request to rent an Item from a Lister. |
| **Lister** | A user who creates a listing for an Item they own, making it available to rent. |
| **Item** | Any physical good listed on the Platform for rental. |
| **Rental Period** | The agreed start and end date (and, where applicable, hours) for which an Item is rented. |
| **Listing** | The posted details of an Item available for rent, created by a Lister. |

## 7. Transactions Between Users

- A rental transaction begins when a Borrower submits a request and a Lister approves it, forming an agreed Rental Period.
- **Borrowers** are expected to return the Item on or before the agreed end date of the Rental Period, in the condition it was received.
- **Listers** are expected to honor the agreed Rental Period and provide the Item as described in the Listing.
- If either party fails to follow the agreed dates or terms of the transaction, the other party may flag the issue to Hiram for review. Hiram may, at its discretion, take action against the account responsible — including disabling the listing or the account involved.
- Hiram is not responsible for enforcing the rental agreement between Borrower and Lister but may act to protect the integrity of the Platform.
